On the Rust/WASM core, a `bench_psu` in current limit reads closer to the truth in the default rail mode. A current-limit step on a rail with no capacitor of its own and one stepped part now takes TR-BDF2, as the exact rail mode does, instead of backward Euler (a 5 V / 20 mA supply into 100 ohm and 10 ohm with 1 uF read 13.8 times the tolerance off at 1 ms; it now reads within 0.005 of it), and a supply that starts in current limit on such a rail no longer holds its first step at the setpoint (it read 4.94 V where the rail was 0.04 V). Leaving current limit no longer publishes a spurious current through a capacitor loop that a bench supply's rail closes (some such boards declined with `bench-psu-back-driven`). On 707 random bench-supply boards that read past the tolerance, 262 now read within it against an independent reference (142 before), 168 read closer, 9 read slightly farther (each on a board already well off, or in a case this change does not cover: rail capacitors and several stepped parts on one limited rail stay as before), and none that read within the tolerance leaves it. Readings of such boards differ from 0.7.0; boards whose supply never reaches its limit read as before.
